Who Was Charlie Kirk?

Charlie Kirk was an American conservative political activist, author, and media personality.

At 18, he co-founded Turning Point USA and became widely known for campus debates, public speaking, and political commentary.

His Ideas & Mottos

  • Freedom and individual responsibility
  • Political engagement among young people
  • Conservative and Christian values
  • “The best way to fight bad ideas is with better ideas.”

What Happened?

On September 10, 2025, Charlie Kirk was speaking at an event at Utah Valley University in Orem, Utah, when he was fatally shot. He was 31 years old.

His death received widespread attention and renewed discussions about political violence in the United States.
